What is 'Velaphi?

- Velaphi is a website that helps people find out more information concerning their surnames, revealing history and the clan names associated with that surname. Further than that, users might find out about well known people who have shared the same surname, past/present. I hope this can empower people in some way. - The idea for the website came from me wishing there was a credible and user friendly website I could discover to about more about my surname and izithakazelo zakhona. - This is for my personal portfolio, **highlighting my use of html, css and javascript**, but I hope to release it to the public, hopefully be able to scale it up with proper funding and research backed by institutions for credibility.
