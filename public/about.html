What is 'Velaphi?

'Velaphi - Where do you come from?

Velaphi is a website that helps you learn more about your surname: the history behind it, the clan names (izithakazelo) that belong to it, and the well-known people, past and present, who share it. I hope it can empower people in some way, even if only by bringing them a little closer to where they come from.

The idea began with a wish of my own. I wanted a credible, user-friendly place where I could discover more about my surname and its izithakazelo and I couldn’t find one. So I decided to make one.

For now, Velaphi is a personal portfolio project, built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express, and MySQL. But I hope to release it to the public. With proper funding, and research backed by institutions for credibility, I’d love to scale it into a resource people can trust for all the different tribes that exist in South Africa.
